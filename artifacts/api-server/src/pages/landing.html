Questions we get asked.

Do I need a special camera?

No. Any phone camera, filmed side-on, whole body in frame, ten seconds or longer. No markers and no wearables.

Is this an injury screen?

No. We measure joint angles against published bands and tell you where you sat outside them. We don’t diagnose, and we don’t claim to prevent injury.

Will the same clip give the same score twice?

Yes. Scores are computed from measured angles, not written by a model from your session title. Same clip, same numbers.

What happens to my video?

Measurement runs on your device. Deleting a session removes the local video with it. Your profile photo is device-local and never uploaded.

Who can sign up?

Thirteen and over. Sign-up asks for a date of birth for that reason.

When does it ship?

iOS first. Join the waitlist and you’ll get one email when TestFlight opens — not a newsletter.
